MTech, Computational and Data Science, NIT Karnataka. Small apps I use every day. Each one runs in the browser and keeps its data on your device.

  • Kitchen From Zero/Kitchen A phone-first meal planner for a small kitchen with no fridge or oven: recipes across seven cuisines with nutrition, pantry-based cooking and daily plans.
